Who else receives data

  • The shop you reserve from sees your reservation and pickup code, but not your email address.
  • Hosting: [hosting provider, country], which stores the app and its database for us.
  • Email: [email provider], which delivers password reset emails.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom): place names you search are sent to its address search, and the map pictures are loaded from its servers, which see your network address.

Stored on your device

Food Rescue keeps your login and a few choices (last place searched, distance, language) in your browser's storage. They are needed for the app to work, so no consent banner is needed. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download and delete your data yourself in Account settings (tap your initials at the top). You also have the right to restrict or object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to [privacy contact email] for anything else; we answer within one month.

You can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or the authority where you live.

Security

Passwords are stored as slow, salted hashes. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Repeated wrong passwords are slowed down, and changing your password signs out your other devices.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
