1. About Food Rescue

Food Rescue, run by [company name] ([registered address], [registration number]), lets shops offer food they could not sell at a lower price, and lets people reserve it and collect it at the shop. We provide the platform; the sale is between you and the shop.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least [16] years old. Give a real name and an email address you can read.
  • Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else used your account, change your password: this signs out every other device.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Account settings.

3. Reserving and collecting food

  • A reservation holds the food for you until the pickup time shown. Collect it by then and show your pickup code; after that it may go back on sale.
  • You pay the shop when you collect, using the payment methods the shop accepts. The price shown in the app is the price you pay.
  • Cancel in the app if you can't come, so someone else can rescue the food. Reserving without collecting again and again may lead to your account being limited.
  • Surprise bags contain a mix of what the shop has left; their contents can't be chosen in advance.

4. Food safety and allergies

  • Each shop is responsible for the food it offers: that it is safe, correctly described, and stored and handled according to food safety law.
  • Descriptions may be short, and some may have been suggested by software and checked by the shop. If you have an allergy or intolerance, ask the shop about ingredients and allergens before you eat the food.
  • Pay attention to "use by" dates and eat food soon after collecting it.

5. Free Pay-It-Forward items

Some items are donated for people going through a hard time. Only verified accounts can claim them, at most [one] per day. Please claim only if you need it, so the help reaches the people it is meant for.

6. Shops
